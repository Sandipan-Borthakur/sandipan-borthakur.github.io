Sandipan Borthakur

Hello! Welcome to my webpage. I am Sandipan Borthakur. I am from Assam, India, best known for its tea and the home to one-horned rhinos! I am a fourth- and final-year PhD student at TU Graz, Austria, and the University of Tartu, Estonia. I did both my Bachelor's in Physics and my Master's in Physics (specialisation in Astronomy and Astrophysics) from the University of Delhi, India.

My research focuses on studying the composition of the inner regions of the protoplanetary discs using the surface composition of their host-stars. Since all young stars accrete material from their inner protoplanetary discs, the accreted material can alter the composition of the surface of the star. This effect is prominent in Herbig Ae/Be stars which are pre main sequence stars with temperatures hotter than ~ 6500 K. These stars either have a thin convective envelope or a fully radiative envelope where the accreted material onto the star do not get mixed efficiently with the stellar interior leaving a long-lasting imprint on the photosphere of these stars. Thus, the photospheres of Herbig Ae/Be stars act as a tracer of their inner protoplanetary disc composition. In my present work, I am exploiting this idea to study the chemical evolution of the inner discs for different elements like oxygen, carbon and others.
